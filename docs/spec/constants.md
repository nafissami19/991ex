## 2. Metric Conversions (`SHIFT 8 (CONV)`)

Based on NIST Special Publication 811 (2008). Conversion syntax: `{value} {Command}`.

### 1: Length
- `1: in ▸ cm`: $x \times 2.54$
- `2: cm ▸ in`: $x / 2.54$
- `3: ft ▸ m`: $x \times 0.3048$
- `4: m ▸ ft`: $x / 0.3048$
- `5: yd ▸ m`: $x \times 0.9144$
- `6: m ▸ yd`: $x / 0.9144$
- `7: mile ▸ km`: $x \times 1.609344$
- `8: km ▸ mile`: $x / 1.609344$
- `9: n mile ▸ m`: $x \times 1852$
- `A: m ▸ n mile`: $x / 1852$
- `B: pc ▸ km`: $x \times 3.085677581 \times 10^{13}$
- `C: km ▸ pc`: $x / 3.085677581 \times 10^{13}$

### 2: Area
- `1: acre ▸ m²`: $x \times 4046.8564224$
- `2: m² ▸ acre`: $x / 4046.8564224$

### 3: Volume
- `1: gal(US) ▸ L`: $x \times 3.785411784$
- `2: L ▸ gal(US)`: $x / 3.785411784$
- `3: gal(UK) ▸ L`: $x \times 4.54609$
- `4: L ▸ gal(UK)`: $x / 4.54609$

### 4: Mass
- `1: oz ▸ g`: $x \times 28.349523125$
- `2: g ▸ oz`: $x / 28.349523125$
- `3: lb ▸ kg`: $x \times 0.45359237$
- `4: kg ▸ lb`: $x / 0.45359237$

### 5: Velocity (Page ▼ 1)
- `1: km/h ▸ m/s`: $x \times \frac{1000}{3600} = x / 3.6$
- `2: m/s ▸ km/h`: $x \times 3.6$

### 6: Pressure (Page ▼ 2)
- `1: atm ▸ Pa`: $x \times 101325$
- `2: Pa ▸ atm`: $x / 101325$
- `3: mmHg ▸ Pa`: $x \times 133.322387415$
- `4: Pa ▸ mmHg`: $x / 133.322387415$
- `5: kgf/cm² ▸ Pa`: $x \times 98066.5$
- `6: Pa ▸ kgf/cm²`: $x / 98066.5$
- `7: lbf/in² ▸ kPa`: $x \times 6.894757293168$
- `8: kPa ▸ lbf/in²`: $x / 6.894757293168$

### 7: Energy (Page ▼ 3)
- `1: kgf·m ▸ J`: $x \times 9.80665$
- `2: J ▸ kgf·m`: $x / 9.80665$
- `3: J ▸ cal`: $x / 4.1855$ ($15^\circ\text{C}$ calorie)
- `4: cal ▸ J`: $x \times 4.1855$

### 8: Power (Page ▼ 4)
- `1: hp ▸ kW`: $x \times 0.74569987158227022$
- `2: kW ▸ hp`: $x / 0.74569987158227022$

### 9: Temperature (Page ▼▼ 1)
- `1: °F ▸ °C`: $(x - 32) \times \frac{5}{9}$
- `2: °C ▸ °F`: $x \times \frac{9}{5} + 32$
